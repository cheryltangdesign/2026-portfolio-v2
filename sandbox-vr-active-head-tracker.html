✦ 01 Overview

My goal was to design a compact and comfortable active head tracker that could be integrated into Sandbox VR's immersive gaming system.

The design needed to support accurate tracking while remaining comfortable, durable and visually consistent with the Sandbox VR ecosystem.

Tools

✦ 02 Moodboard

The exploration of form and visual direction focused on creating a compact, technical and approachable object.

Different references were explored to establish the visual language and interaction principles for the product.

✦ 03 Sketches & Prototypes

Early sketches explored different mounting approaches, proportions and interaction points.

Physical prototypes were then used to evaluate the form and positioning of the tracker.

✦ 04 Vive Focus 3

The tracker was developed around the Vive Focus 3, considering the headset geometry, mounting position and user comfort.

Multiple iterations were tested to determine the most effective placement and attachment strategy.

✦ 05 Features

The final concept incorporated several key features designed to improve comfort, stability and ease of use.

✦ 06 Quality Testing

Testing focused on fit, durability and the reliability of the tracking system during repeated use.

✦07 CMF Selection

Different clip configurations were evaluated to determine the most secure and practical mounting solution.

✦08 Accessible UI

The physical interface and interaction points were refined to make the product intuitive and accessible during use.

✦ 09 Final Design

The final design combines the tracking hardware with a lightweight mounting system designed for comfort, stability and repeatable use.

  • Compact form factor
  • Secure head mounting
  • Comfortable fit
  • Integrated tracking hardware
  • Production-ready construction
