Active Head Tracker

A wearable head tracking system designed for immersive VR experiences, focusing on accurate tracking, comfort and integration with existing hardware.

Role Industrial & UX Designer
Timeline 2022 – 2026
Tools

✦ 01 Overview

The design needed to support accurate tracking while remaining comfortable, durable and visually consistent with the Sandbox VR ecosystem.

My goal was to design a compact and comfortable active head tracker that could be integrated into Sandbox VR's immersive gaming system.

✦ 02 Moodboard

Futuristic - Cyberpunk - Sleek - Rugged - Durable

The combination of matte and glossy finishes, accented by illuminated coloured player LED strips, creates a high-tech and futuristic aesthetic.

✦ 03 Sketches & Prototypes

Early sketches explored different mounting approaches, proportions and interaction points.

Physical prototypes were then used to evaluate the form and positioning of the tracker.

✦ 04 Vive Focus 3

​The Active Head Tracker is designed to integrate seamlessly with the HTC Vive Focus 3 without compromising its performance, comfort, or usability.

To deliver a reliable and smooth product lifecycle - deployment > gear on > in-game experience > gear off > charging > cleaning > maintenance for both users and operators.

✦ 05 Features

Low latency streaming

Active IR tracking solution improves tracking accuracy and enables lower-latency motion capture for fast-paced VR experiences. It also delivers more reliable performance in large multiplayer arenas, with fewer tracking dropouts and less interference from ambient lighting.

Wireless solution

Upgraded from bucky tethered gaming PC backpacks to standalone wireless headsets, enables true free-roam movements and a more immersive gameplay experience, so as to minimise motion sickness for players.

✦ 06 Quality Testing

Worked closely with hardware engineers to design unique marker patterns for different players.


The parameters of active LEDs were extensively tested and optimized in real in-store environments, which significantly improved detection accuracy and tracking stability under varying lighting conditions.

✦ 07 CMF Selection

Darkened IR-transmitting polycarbonate is selected for its high impact strength and heat resistance, while effectively concealing the internal components.

A glossy surface finish to minimise IR refraction, hence maximising transmission efficiency.

✦ 08 Accessible UI

The physical interface and interaction points were refined to make the product intuitive and accessible during use.

✦ 09 Final Design

The final design combines the tracking hardware with a lightweight mounting system designed for comfort and stability.

- Compact form factor
- Secure head mounting
- Comfortable fit
- Integrated tracking hardware
- Production-ready construction
