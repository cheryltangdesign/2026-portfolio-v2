✦ 01 Overview

A new family of wireless headphones for adults and kids that extends Motorola’s headphone lineup while retaining the signature S-curves of the Pulse Escape side bands.

✦ 02 Back-to-back sessions

Two independent sets of VR equipment Gear A / Gear B are setup within a single arena Stage 1 / Stage 2 to support back-to-back sessions.

This setup improves operational and cost efficiency while preventing software-linked equipment from being mixed between sessions.

White for Stage 1 and black for Stage 2, visual identifiers such as stickers, velcro patches, and 3D printed attachments are added to existing Sandbox VR equipment to help staff quickly and easily distinguish between the two sets of gear.
