Motorola Headphones

A new family of wireless bluetooth headphones for adults and kids that extends Motorola’s headphone lineup.

✦ 01 Overview

An extended family of headphones inspired by and pay tribute to Motorola's signature Pulse Escape headphone series.

✦ 02 The S-curve

The signature S-curves on the Pulse Escape side bands are retained, creating a recognizable visual connection across the new range.

The design explores how this established form language can be adapted across different sizes and user needs while maintaining a cohesive family identity.

✦ 03 Wireless Bluetooth Headphone

The Escape 200 has upgraded the side panel with a metal finish and a tone-on-tone silk-screened logo, giving the headphones a more refined and premium appearance.

The subtle treatment elevates the material quality while maintaining the clean, understated character of the overall design.

✦ 04 Kids Wireless Headphone

The Squads 300 balances a fun, approachable character with a clean and durable form, making it well suited for younger users and shared listening experiences.

Featuring a Buddy Jack, the design allows two headphones to connect and share audio, adding a simple and playful element to the listening experience.
