MIMO App

The MIMO UI ecosystem consists of three connected products: MIMO Phone, MIMO Watch, and the Parent’s Phone.

Working together to create a seamless experience between kids and parents.

✦ 01 The Ecosystem

The MIMO UI ecosystem connects three products — MIMO Phone, MIMO Watch, and Parent’s Phone — into one seamless experience.

Together, they help kids stay connected, active, safe, and independent, while keeping parents informed and involved.

✦ 02 Core features

A simple, connected smartphone and smartwatch experience designed around four key needs: communication, safety, healthy habits, and financial learning.

Parents can guide, support, and reward their kids through simple controls and shared experiences.

✦ 03 User Flow

The ecosystem creates a continuous connection between kids and parents.

Everyday activities, from sending a photo or checking in, to completing a health challenge or receiving an allowance, can be shared instantly across MIMO devices. Keeping both sides connected without adding complexity.

✦ 04 Navigation

A clear, playful, and intuitive interface designed for kids.

Consistent interactions across MIMO devices make everyday tasks simple, while giving parents quick access to the information that matters.

✦ 05 Instant sharing

One button, instant connection.

Kids can quickly share selfies, photos, and voice messages with parents and buddies, making communication simple, fast, and fun.

✦ 06 Health tracker

Parents can create personalised challenges based on sport type and duration, while MIMO tracks progress through the phone and watch.

Completing challenges earns stars, which can be exchanged for extra screen time or monetary rewards. Turning healthy activities into rewarding challenges and positive habits.

✦ 07 Digital wallet

MIMO introduces kids to the basics of earning, saving, budgeting, and spending through a simple digital wallet, helping them develop financial independence from an early age.
