✦ 01 The Ecosystem

By linking between various platforms - interactive hardware and devices, children can instantly and seamlessly share their happy moments with their parents and buddies.

Also the health tracker monitors statistics like heart rate and sport duration, parents can set personalised exercise goals for their kids. Integrating with the reward system to help their kids form regular and healthy habits.

✦ 02 Core features

A simple phone experience for kids that helps them stay connected, build healthy habits, learn about money, and stay safe.

✦ 03 User Flow

A connected kids’ ecosystem that brings communication, safety, healthy habits, rewards, and money education together.

Allowing kids and parents to instantly share updates, achievements, location, and important moments, while giving parents simple controls and kids a safe, engaging experience.

✦ 04 Navigation

✦ 05 Instant sharing

Media such as selfies, photos and voice messages can be shared via a single button, letting young users connect with their parents and buddies with an easier and faster experience.

✦ 06 Health tracker

Customisable challenges promote healthier lifestyles for the kids.

Different combinations of sport types and durations determine the challenge's difficulty and amount of star reward the kid receives. The young users will be rewarded with stars upon completing the exercise, with the aid of the MIMO devices.

The star reward system can be exchanged as an increase of screen time or monetary reward, and help form good habits through monetary and non-monetary incentives.

✦ 07 Wallet

An early financial education through earning, saving, budgeting, and spending digitally.
