Hubble Growth

A modern baby scale designed to provide a comfortable, safe, and intuitive weighing experience. The design combines a soft, supportive weighing pad with a clean, minimal base, creating a calm product experience that fits naturally into contemporary homes.

✦ 01 Overview

The design focuses on ergonomics, comfort, ease of use, and visual simplicity.

Rounded edges and a soft-touch weighing surface provide a secure and gentle experience, while the compact form makes the scale easy to store and handle.

✦ 02 Elevated Accessibility

A sleek, streamlined scale body creates a clean and modern aesthetic while maintaining a compact, lightweight form.

An elevated screen and control interface improve visibility and accessibility, allowing caregivers to read measurements and operate the scale with ease.

✦ 03 Sleek & Portable

An easy-grip form makes the scale comfortable to carry, while the dotted texture on the bottom provides added grip and stability during handling.

✦ 04 Guided Comfort

The scale and pad share a curved form that naturally guides the baby toward the center, creating a secure and comfortable weighing position.

Large soft pad covers the whole scale for full comfort.
