✦ 01 Overview

URBAN COMPOSITION is a graphic design project created in collaboration with a friend for the University College London end-of-year exhibition.

The project showcased work produced by students from the 2017/2018 MSc Urban Design and City Planning programme, translating their diverse research and design projects into a cohesive visual identity for the exhibition.

✦ 02 Core design concept

Each element represents a building block of the city, derived from recurring themes explored throughout the students’ projects and dissertations, including streets, social inclusion, green spaces, and neighbourhoods.
