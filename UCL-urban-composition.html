✦ 01 Overview

URBAN COMPOSITION is a graphic design project done in collaboration with a friend of mine who studied in UCL for their end-of-year expo, showcasing the work produced by the 2017/2018 MSc Urban Design and City Planning students.

✦ 02 Core design concept

Each element — a building block of a city, is derived from themes common throughout students’ projects and dissertations, such as the street, social inclusion in the city, green spaces, the neighbourhood.
