MaskFone

Combines protection, convenience, and technology. It features replaceable PM2.5 and N95/FFP2 filters, a built-in microphone, and earphones, reducing the need to remove mask in public.

Company:
Hubble Connected

Year:
2020

Duration:
6 Months

Tools:

✦ 01 Overview

The COVID-19 pandemic made face masks an essential part of everyday life, creating new challenges for people who needed to communicate while wearing one.

The goal of this project was to design a solution that enables users to make and receive phone calls clearly and comfortably while wearing a face mask.

✦ 02 Problem

Existing solutions, such as wired and wireless earbuds, are often worn outside the face mask, resulting in unclear audio and, in the case of wired earbuds, cable entanglement with the mask.

This can require users to repeatedly adjust or remove the mask during phone calls.

✦ 03 Fabric

MaskFone uses a soft twill fabric that conforms comfortably to the contours of the face. Its tightly woven structure helps block larger particles while allowing airflow, providing a comfortable fit while helping reduce exposure to airborne pathogens.

✦ 04 N95 filters

The replaceable N95 filters catch 95% of particles. The combination cuts the viral load significantly. The filters also remove bacteria and pollutant, ensuring the air breathed in will be fresh and pure.

✦ 05 Onboard friendly

Compatible with standard airline audio adapters and 3.5 mm cables, allowing users to enjoy audio while travelling.

✦ 06 Sport version

An added ventilation valve improves airflow and breathability during physical activity, providing greater comfort during extended wear.

✦ 07 Final design

MaskFone integrates an audio solution directly into the face mask, allowing users to make and receive calls without the inconvenience of adjusting or removing their mask in public.
