Nursery Pal Glow

A smart, customisable, and remotely controlled baby monitor that features remote pan / tilt / zoom, enabling parents to keep an eye on the baby anytime, anywhere from a smartphone.

Comapny:

Hubble Connected


Year:

2019 - 2020


Duration:

9 Months


Tools:

✦ 01 Overview

XXX noise cancelling earbuds for Nokia, creating a seamless and engaging listening experience for an active lifestyle.

✦ 02 Moodboard

Minimal - Rounded - Clean - Sleek - Fun

✦ 03 Sketches

The soft and rounded shape, and the white textured housing make it up for a comfortable and cozy appearance that blends in with the baby's room.

✦ 04 Nightlight

​A 7-colour customisable nightlight is integrated into the product base, where the light refracts onto the table surface to create a soft, diffused glow.

Designed to support healthy sleep routines, it helps little ones develop consistent sleeping habits.

✦ 05 Pan & Tilt

A ball-shaped head paired with a rounded base enables smooth digital Pan / Tilt movement while keeping the overall footprint compact.

For wide-angle viewing, where the housing can easily enter the camera’s field of view, the front housing curves beneath the lens to maximise the vertical viewing range.

✦ 06 Exploded view

Featuring two integrated motors for a wider field of vision and flexible monitoring.

An integrated array of IR LEDs provides clear visibility in both day and night conditions.

✦ 07 Detailing

Speaker

Circular dot pattern matches rounded form of the camera.

Metallic ring

A premium touch, highlights camera and IR lens.

✦ 08 Final Design

A sleek yet simple baby monitor, designed to give parents greater control and flexibility:

- Wi-Fi connectivity with smart devices
- 1080p Full HD video
- Smooth pan / tilt / zoom
- Two-way talk
- 7-color nightlight sleep trainer
