Micro Earbuds Pro

A compact earbuds design that fits ergonomically in ears for greater comfort and better sound, while minimising its size.

Comapny:

Nokia


Year:

2021


Duration:

6 Months


Tools:

✦ 01 Overview

The goal was to design compact, comfortable, true wireless active noise cancelling earbuds for Nokia, creating a seamless and engaging listening experience for an active lifestyle.

✦ 02 Moodboard

Ergonomic - Soft - Pocketable - Harmonious - Urban

Exploring form factors, proportions, and surface transitions to create a cohesive system between the charging case and earbuds. The design focuses on a compact, easy-to-hold form that can be comfortably carried in a pocket for everyday use.

✦ 03 Sketches

The functionality of a product often determines its form, especially for a delicate product like earbuds. To balance both aspects, a design should begin with the research on user pain points for existing products.

Comfortability is one of the elements a user cares most when finding a pair of earbuds. Therefore paying attention to factors like - product form, fitting, weight, sweat and rain - when designing would help maximise fundamental functions without compromising its visually appealing aspects.

✦ 04 Ergonomic earbuds

Ergonomic design

Everyone’s ears and ear canals are different in sizes and shapes, so even if those one-size-fits-all earbuds are made according to “standardised” measures, it will not fit every person perfectly.

The tapered and rounded fit helps form a tight but comfortable seal for a better noise isolation.

Ergonomic testing

Apart from user testing, pressure mapping assessment was also undertaken to help identify areas on the earbuds that tend to be high or low pressures, hence quantify comfort.

✦ 05 Super slim case

​The Active Head Tracker is designed to integrate seamlessly with the HTC Vive Focus 3 without compromising its performance, comfort, or usability.

To deliver a reliable and smooth product lifecycle - deployment > gear on > in-game experience > gear off > charging > cleaning > maintenance for both users and operators.

✦ 06 Build to last

​Discreet design with a durable build.

✦ 07 Exploded view

Working around delicate internal components, including the touch sensor and PCB, while maximising battery capacity within a compact form. The design balances internal constraints with the need to keep the earbuds small, comfortable, and functional.

✦ 08 IPX5

Perfect for outdoors. The earbuds are waterproof, sweatproof, and workout friendly.
