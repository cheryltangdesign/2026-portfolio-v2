Privacy Screen

✦ 01 Overview

Industrial and graphic design project - a modular outdoor privacy screen system developed for Wall Theory. The system was designed to create comfortable, visually appealing spaces while providing shade and privacy.

The project aimed to balance architectural aesthetics, durability, and ease of installation for residential and commercial environments.

✦ 02 Patterns

Sculptural - Functional - Leisure - Modern - Weatherproof

✦ 03 Full size screens

​Exploring geometric and organic pattern directions, refining them for visual balance, privacy coverage, manufacturability, and performance.

✦ 04 Modular system

Screen styles are designed to work within the modular panel system, making it easy to create modern privacy walls, patio dividers, deck screens, and equipment enclosures.

Each laser-cut pattern offers a different balance of privacy, airflow, and architectural design.

✦ 05 Size variations

A more compact 40" tall alternative for areas where full 68" coverage isn't needed.

Ideal for concealing outdoor equipment and creating low partitions.

✦ 06 Colour variations

Available in a range of versatile colours — black / dark bronze /white, the system can be customised to complement different architectural styles and outdoor environments, from bold contemporary finishes to subtle, natural tones.
